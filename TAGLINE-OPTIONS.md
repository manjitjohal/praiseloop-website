# PraiseLoop Homepage Tagline — Options & Recommendation

**Date:** 2026-08-07  ·  **Owner:** Manjit  ·  **For decision:** Ash / founder
**Status:** Exploring — current live H1 is *"Rewards are earned, not given."*

The live H1 is clean but close to category language ("earn rewards" is what
every points platform says). Full option set below, grouped by angle —
blue-sky is the preferred direction.

## Why the H1 can afford to be blue-sky

It doesn't work alone — the hero stacks three layers:

| Layer | Copy | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Kicker | AI-Powered **Performance** · **Recognition** · **Rewards** | Category (Dunford) |
| **H1** | *← the line we're choosing* | The idea / the feeling |
| Wedge subline | *"If the KPI didn't happen, the reward doesn't exist."* | Differentiator |

**Guardrails:** don't lead with *recognition* or *effort* · no *culture*
framing · must survive being said out loud · ideally uncopyable by competitors.

## The options

Angles: **Blue-sky** (the idea — preferred direction) · **Double meaning**
(buyer and employee both hear their line) · **Enemy** (category reframe) ·
**Buyer** (retention emotion) · **Mechanism** (most literal — set aside as too cold).

| Line | Angle | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Winning, on loop.** ⭐ | Blue-sky | Two words; makes the brand name mean something; compounding implied |
| **Great work shouldn't be a secret.** ⭐ | Blue-sky | The execution gap felt, not described; the most human |
| Winning is contagious. | Blue-sky | Bold; literally the product thesis (peer flywheel) |
| Every win starts the next one. | Blue-sky | The flywheel as a belief; warm |
| Put winning on repeat. | Blue-sky | Imperative "on loop"; more energy, less elegance |
| Applause, with receipts. | Blue-sky | Praise + proof; uncopyable, but cheekiest tone — wildcard |
| Make performance pay. | Double meaning | Pays the employee (coins) and the company (ROI) |
| Reward what works. | Double meaning | Behaviours that work + rewards that provably work (proof layer) |
| Pay for performance. Literally. | Double meaning | Comp cliché made concrete; cheeky |
| Performance is a loop, not a review. | Enemy | Names the enemy (annual review); activates the brand name |
| Performance isn't annual anymore. | Enemy | Same enemy, flatter — better as a subline |
| Not another recognition platform. A performance loop. | Enemy | Dunford said out loud; long for an H1 |
| Reward the results, not the noise. | Enemy | Frames the category as noise |
| Keep your best people winning. | Buyer | Retention + performance in five words |
| Catch people winning. | Buyer | Flips "catch people slacking"; explains the AI Deputy; most personality |
| No result, no reward. | Mechanism | Hardest four words; reads stick-not-carrot |
| The result lands. The reward fires. | Mechanism | Cause→effect in two beats; vending-machine |
| Hit the number. Keep the reward. | Mechanism | Second person, benefit-forward |
| Rewards you can't game. | Mechanism | Kills nomination-form rivals |
| If it didn't happen, it doesn't pay. | Mechanism | Conversational echo of the wedge line |
| Reward performance you can prove. | Mechanism | Pure Dunford; reads like a feature |
| Recognition that pays for itself. | Mechanism | CFO framing but recognition-led (guardrail) |
| Win. Get rewarded. Repeat. | Mechanism | Mantra-shaped; too gamified for C-suite — save for field-worker page |
