## 1. The decision in one line

Positioning locks to **"AI-Powered Performance, Recognition & Rewards."**
One wedge, led by the KPI→coin differentiator, with **the loop carrying the
breadth** — so we get all three pillars without reading as an everything-platform.

---

## 2. Why — the positioning logic

Two briefs pulled in opposite directions and had to be reconciled:

- **Founder (site walkthrough):** the site reads as *"just recognition"* → wants
  **three pillars** (performance, recognition, reward) plus the AI layer made
  visible. Instinct: *add breadth.*
- **Positioning critique (Slack, 10:36):** *"Category sprawl — three categories in
  a trench coat."* One category, one wedge. Drop "Workforce Intelligence." Instinct:
  *cut to one wedge.* (Dunford: if the buyer can't place you, they can't shortlist you.)

Taken literally, these collide. Promoting performance + recognition + reward +
intelligence to co-equal headline categories rebuilds exactly the sprawling blurb
the critique rejected.

**Resolution — separate *category* from *scope*:**

- **Category** (the H1 / what the buyer files us under): ONE wedge — narrow,
  memorable, singular.
- **Scope** (what the platform spans): performance, recognition, reward, intelligence
  — but presented as **one loop, not four categories.**

The **loop is the device** that lets the founder have his breadth without the sprawl:
four capabilities described as four things = trench coat; the same four described as
**stages of one flywheel** = a single differentiated position. "How it loops back to
the manager — that's what nobody's doing" is also the founder's own closing ask, so
the loop satisfies both briefs.

**Consequences of this frame:**

- **Drop "Workforce Intelligence"** as a named category anywhere on the page. The
  AI-analysis capability stays — framed as part of the loop.
- **Elevate the buried differentiator:** *"If the KPI didn't happen, the reward
  doesn't exist."* This is the line prospects repeat internally; today it's absent
  from the page.
- **Sell to the buyer** (leaders / C-suite), not the employee. Tie to performance,
  retention, absenteeism, cost of underperformance — not culture/peer framing. (We
  made the opposite mistake with SupportRoom: too employee-focused, HR didn't connect.)
- **Keep the mantra:** *Measure. Recognise. Reward. Improve. Repeat. — Performance
  isn't managed once a year, it's optimised every day.*

## 3. Change list

### Positioning (locks the frame for everything else)

- Title/positioning: **"AI-Powered Performance, Recognition & Rewards."** One wedge —
  **drop "Workforce Intelligence"** as a named category.
- Lead with the differentiator, not generic AI claims: **"If the KPI didn't happen,
  the reward doesn't exist"** becomes a hero centrepiece. *(Net-new — currently
  absent/buried.)*
- Rebalance the whole page off *recognition-only* — performance / recognition /
  reward shown as **stages of one loop**, not three separate pitches. *(Concrete:
  five recognition-heavy headlines to rework — see §4.)*
- Speak to the **buyer (leaders / C-suite)**: performance, retention, absenteeism,
  cost of underperformance — not employee/culture framing.
- Keep the close: *Measure. Recognise. Reward. Improve. Repeat.*

### Homepage structure

- New hero: **remove the "AI Deputy" demo card** (the busy "$48K deal" mini-dashboard
  reads dense/techie), put a **team photo** there; the agent demo moves further down,
  done better.
- Add the **loop diagram** — manager → agent → employee → performance/recognition/
  reward → back to manager. *(Net-new — no such visual exists; only a flat 1–4 list.
  This is the thing Lattice etc. don't show.)*
- Give the **AI agent its own prominent section**: what it does, the value, the ROI —
  with a visual of it working between manager and employee.
- Rename **"Outcome Engine" → "Outcome Coin Engine."**
- **Remove the 01/02/03/04 section numbering.**
- Reframe the "Culture" section → **behaviours → performance → retention/absenteeism/cost.**
- Add a **hiring/turnover angle** to the ROI and buyer numbers.
- Reword the headlines flagged as too techie (the execution-gap H2 and "Your systems
  in…") into plainer, performance-led language.
- **Cut, don't just space out:** the page is very long (~10k px) and repeats
  "recognition." Reframe/merge redundant sections (e.g. "Culture is group behaviour"
  + the recognition feed) — serves both "easy read" and the one-wedge goal.

### Design polish

- **Keywords** (performance / recognition / reward) highlighted in **orange**.
- Secondary CTA styled **orange** (not a grey line).
- Integrations line: **"Zero integrations required"** on its own line and bigger;
  supporting text underneath.
- More whitespace, less "clawed," **numbered steps in circles** *(currently a tight,
  un-circled, un-titled list)*, general de-cluttering.

### Scope — separate landing pages, not the homepage

- Homepage goes **global / multi-language**, not GCC/Arabic-heavy *(GCC section is
  currently very region-specific)*.
- Dedicated landing pages to follow: **UAE/GCC**, **blue-collar/field workers**, and
  one each for **performance / recognition / reward**.

## 4. Verified against the live site (2026-08-05)

- **Recognition repetition is visible at headline-scan level** — five hits:
  "Managers know **recognition** matters" → "**Recognition** is forgotten" → "Never
  the **recognition**" → "AI **recognition** that runs itself" → "peer
  **recognition**." The rebalance is these five plus the hero.
- **The KPI→coin differentiator is genuinely absent** — closest is a buried body line
  ("every coin ties back to a real result"). Elevating it is net-new hero copy.
- **The loop diagram doesn't exist** — there's a flow *list* (1–4) and a small "every
  result feeds the next nudge" tag, but no single manager→agent→employee→pillars→loop
  visual. The founder's centrepiece ask is a from-scratch build.
- **Page is ~10k px tall** — "space it out" and "cut the recognition repetition" point
  the same way: reframe/merge, don't just pad.

## 5. Reference — the positioning copy this brief builds on

> **PraiseLoop | AI-Powered Performance Recognition & Rewards**
>
> PraiseLoop is the platform where rewards are earned, not given. It plugs into the
> systems where work is already measured — when a verified KPI result lands,
> recognition and reward trigger automatically. If the result didn't happen, the
> reward doesn't exist.
>
> Around that engine, AI continuously analyses performance, KPIs, behaviours and
> engagement, giving managers real-time intelligence on what's driving high
> performance and where support is needed. AI agents surface the moments worth
> recognising, flag when someone's slipping, and recommend coaching actions — so every
> employee knows where they stand and how to improve.
>
> The result is a continuous performance loop that turns workforce data into measurable
> outcomes: engagement improves, performance accelerates, top talent stays, and the
> cost of underperformance falls.
>
> *Measure. Recognise. Reward. Improve. Repeat.*
> *Performance isn't managed once a year. It's optimised every day.*
